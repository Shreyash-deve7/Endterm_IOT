For the Canny edge detection in the receiver, I chose a lower threshold of 100 and an upper threshold of 200.

The reason behind this choice is the way Canny uses two thresholds. Any pixel with a gradient value above the upper threshold (200) is treated as a strong edge and is kept for sure. Any pixel below the lower threshold (100) is rejected as not being an edge. The pixels that fall in between are kept only if they are connected to a strong edge. By keeping the ratio close to 1:2, which is the commonly recommended range, I get clean and continuous edges while avoiding too much noise from the webcam. A lower threshold value would have picked up a lot of unwanted noise and tiny details, while setting both values too high would have missed real edges. So 100 and 200 give a good balance between detecting proper edges and ignoring background noise in a live video stream.
